Formula 1 Lap Time and Race Strategy Model

Personal project · 2026 · Published and runnable in the browser

  • Built a quasi-steady lap time model in Python on circuit geometry derived from car position telemetry, predicting the 2025 Bahrain pole lap to within 0.72%, 89.20 s against 89.84 s measured, with an 18 km/h speed trace RMSE.
  • Fitted tyre degradation, fuel burn and driver baseline effects simultaneously by least squares across 979 green-flag racing laps, reducing model error from 0.82 s to 0.58 s.
  • Evaluated 14,532 legal race strategies against pit loss measured from the race itself, placing the winning strategy 3.8 s from the calculated optimum and identifying the pit window.
  • Extended it to a Monte Carlo treatment of strategy under uncertainty, covering correlated and independent tyre variation, skewed pit loss, non-uniform safety car hazard and reactive pitting under caution.
  • Ported the solver to JavaScript so the model can be re-parameterised and re-run in a browser.

Fixed-Wing UAV Wing Design · Second-Year Group Project

2024 to 2025 · Aerodynamics and Stability Lead · Ranked 1st in the cohort

  • Largest minimum-to-maximum speed range in the year group: 23.1 m/s predicted in XFLR5 and Python against 19.8 m/s measured in flight.
  • Designed the wing structure as a box spar developed over three test-driven iterations on a purpose-built bending and torsion rig. Second moment of area 13,643 mm4, a second spar adding 11,539 mm4, then cross members closing the box section. Predicted 18.7 mm tip deflection and 1.57° twist against 13.8 mm and 0.42° measured.
  • Selected the aerofoil in XFOIL with forced transition at 0.16c, screening sections above 12% thickness, and chose NACA 2412 after further analysis in XFLR5. Single-slot Fowler flaps at 30% chord and counter-rotating vortex generators to delay stall.
  • Chose the planform taper using a ranking matrix backed by XFLR5 sweeps, with criteria set before the options were evaluated.
  • Produced the Gantt charts and resource plans that kept the project on schedule and delivered it early.
