About

I am in the final year of a four-year integrated MEng in Aeronautics and Astronautics at the University of Southampton, specialising in aerodynamics, and I graduate in July 2027. I finished my third year with a 71.7% first-class average.

My work sits where design meets data. I like problems that start with something physical, a wing, a test rig, an enclosure, and end with a number you can defend. That has meant building a flume and the computer-vision system to measure what happens inside it, writing lap-time and tyre models that change what a race team actually does, and designing parts that had to survive being manufactured and used.

I am looking for graduate roles in motorsport aerodynamics and race strategy.
