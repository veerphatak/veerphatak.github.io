Laboratory & Computational Coursework

  • Wind tunnel testing. Measured pressure distribution over a NACA 0020 aerofoil against angle of attack, boundary layer development, and finite wing lift and drag, then correlated the results against CFD, XFOIL and XFLR5 and analysed the discrepancies.
  • Computational aerodynamics. Implemented a doublet panel method in Python, including an order-of-accuracy study against panel count and a comparison of cosine against uniform panel distributions.
  • Finite element analysis. Mesh convergence study in ANSYS Mechanical from roughly 24,000 to 1.9 million elements, balancing peak stress accuracy against solve time and memory.
  • Aircraft design. Constraint analysis and V-n diagrams built in Python.

About

I am in the final year of a four-year integrated MEng in Aeronautics and Astronautics at the University of Southampton, specialising in aerodynamics, and I graduate in July 2027. I finished my third year with a 71.7% first-class average.

My work sits where design meets data. I like problems that start with something physical, a wing, a test rig, an enclosure, and end with a number you can defend. That has meant building a flume and the computer-vision system to measure what happens inside it, writing lap-time and tyre models that change what a race team actually does, and designing parts that had to survive being manufactured and used.

I am looking for graduate roles in motorsport aerodynamics and race strategy.
