About

I am in the final year of a four-year integrated MEng in Aeronautics and Astronautics at the University of Southampton, specialising in aerodynamics, and I graduate in July 2027. I completed my third year with a 71.7% first-class average.

My work combines design with quantitative analysis. I am most interested in problems that begin with physical hardware, such as a wing, a test rig or an enclosure, and end in a result that can be justified numerically. That has included designing and building a laboratory flume and the measurement system used to analyse it, developing lap-time and tyre degradation models used to inform race team setup decisions, and designing components that had to be manufactured and used in the field.

I am seeking graduate roles in motorsport aerodynamics and race strategy.
